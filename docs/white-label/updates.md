# White-label updates and renewals

Every licence includes six months of updates from the purchase date. An update is a windowsweep release rebuilt and republished under your name: new sections, fixes and protection rules as they change.

| Group | How an update reaches your users |
|---|---|
| Desktop app | A new build on your GitHub Releases page; installed copies find it with your update key and offer it |
| Command-line tool (npm) | A new version of your package; the next `npx` run picks it up |

## After six months

Without a renewal your builds keep working on the last version published for you. Nothing is switched off.

A renewal adds three, six or twelve months of updates, counted from the later of the renewal date and your current end date. Renew on the [updates page](https://windowsweep.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email the licence was issued to. The current renewal prices are on that page.
