# White-label questions

## Is the code not free already?

It is, under MIT, and anyone can build it. The licence pays for the builds, the publishing and the updates under your name, and for the right to use the windowsweep name and logo, which MIT does not grant.

## Do I get a private copy of the source?

There is none. Everything that builds both programs is in the public repository at [github.com/aoneahsan/windowsweep](https://github.com/aoneahsan/windowsweep).

## Is the licence exclusive?

No. Every licence is non-exclusive, and with public code there is no buyout that could make one exclusive.

## What happens after six months?

Your builds keep working on the last version published for you. A renewal of three, six or twelve months starts the updates again. See [Updates and renewals](./updates.md).

## Will SmartScreen warn about my installer?

Yes, unless you buy a code-signing certificate. windowsweep's own installers are not signed with a paid certificate either.

## How is a licence paid for?

Through the payment provider's checkout, opened from the [white-label pricing page](https://windowsweep.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing). The [Terms of Service](https://windowsweep.aoneahsan.com/terms) cover payment.
